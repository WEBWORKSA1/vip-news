## Supporting sources

- **FeedSpot** celebrity magazine directory: used to map the niche and its largest publishers.
- **Exploding Topics** newsletter study: the largest briefings (The Morning 17M, Daily Skimm 7M, Morning Brew 6M, 1440 4M) share one format: short, curated, about five minutes.
- **USPTO records via Justia and Markinton**: "VIP NEWS SERVICE" (application abandoned) and "VIPNEWSNOW" (registration cancelled March 2023). No live identical mark turned up in this search, which is not a clearance opinion.

## What the research decided

1. **Lead with a ranking, not a feed.** The wealth sites with the strongest return visits put a ranked list at the centre. A daily list of the most-read people is unique data nobody else publishes in this form.
2. **Link out, add value on top.** Sites that republish wire text look thin. Headlines link to publishers; the site's own contribution is the ranking, the people-to-headline matching, the tools and the guides.
3. **One sign-up promise.** The best-converting briefings say the same three things: free, short, leave any time.
4. **Mixed revenue.** Ads alone pay poorly for general news. Sponsorship, reader support and contest partners sit beside AdSense.
5. **Skew toward business, wealth and tech.** Finance and business advertising pays several times more per thousand views than entertainment, so those desks get equal weight with celebrity coverage.
