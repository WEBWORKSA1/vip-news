Privacy and cookies

Last updated Friday, November 27, 2026.

This policy explains what information Vip.News handles and why. It applies to this website only.

Information you give us

When you submit a form (joining the list, advertising enquiries, pledges, contest entries, applications or messages) we receive the details you typed. Forms are delivered by FormSubmit, a third-party form service, to the site's operator. We use the details to respond to you and for the purpose stated on the form. We do not sell them.

Information stored in your browser

Your preferences (light or dark theme, text size, chosen desks, quiz scores and whether you dismissed the cookie notice) are saved in your browser's local storage. They stay on your device.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show advertising. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info. More detail is in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Embedded and third-party content

Videos play through YouTube's privacy-enhanced player and load only when you press play. The comparison and born-today tools request public data from the Wikimedia Foundation directly from your browser. Fonts are served by Google Fonts. These services receive your IP address when their content loads and apply their own privacy policies.

Links to other sites

Headlines link to publishers' websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Your choices

You can clear stored preferences by clearing site data in your browser. To ask what form details we hold about you, or to have them deleted, use the contact form.

Children

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page with a new date.
