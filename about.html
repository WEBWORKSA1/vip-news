About Vip.News

The daily briefing on the people who move the world.

Vip.News is an independent news briefing about people. Instead of sorting the day by topic alone, it starts with a question: who is the world reading about, and what did they do?

What you will find here

  • The guest list. A daily ranking of the 100 most-read people on English Wikipedia, with their position compared with the day before.
  • Headlines from six desks. Power, wealth, tech, entertainment, sports and society, gathered from established publishers. Every headline links to the publisher's own article.
  • Video. Recent uploads from official news channels, played through YouTube's own player.
  • Tools. A weekly quiz, a tool that compares public attention on any two people, and a born-today lookup.
  • Guides. Original explainers on reading the news well.

How the site stays current

An automated job refreshes the headlines, ranking, birthdays and videos several times a day and rebuilds every page. The time of the last refresh is shown in the footer.

Independence

Vip.News is not affiliated with any publisher, person or organisation it links to or names. The site is funded by advertising, sponsorship and reader support. Advertisers and supporters have no say over rankings or which headlines appear.
