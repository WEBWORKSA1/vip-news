Contest rules

Official rules for The Guest List Challenge.

  1. No purchase necessary. Entry is free. A donation, pledge or purchase does not improve your chances.
  2. Who can enter. Adults aged 18 or over, where such contests are lawful. The contest is void where prohibited or restricted by law. The site operator and immediate family are not eligible.
  3. How to enter. Complete the current round's quiz and submit the entry form with a valid email address during the entry period shown on the contest page. One entry per person per round.
  4. How winners are chosen. Each round, the winner is drawn at random from entries with the highest quiz score. Where local law requires a contest of skill, the tie-break answer on the entry form decides.
  5. Prizes. The prize for each round is stated on the contest page before the round opens. Prizes cannot be exchanged or transferred. Taxes are the winner's responsibility.
  6. Notification. Winners are contacted at the email address they entered. A winner who does not reply within 14 days forfeits the prize and another winner may be drawn.
  7. Publicity. Winners are announced by first name and country only, and only with their consent.
  8. Your details. Entry details are used to run the contest and contact winners, as described in the privacy policy.
  9. Changes. The operator may amend, suspend or cancel a round if it cannot be run fairly.
  10. Sponsor. The contest is run by the operator of Vip.News and is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by Google, YouTube, the Wikimedia Foundation or any publisher named on the site.
